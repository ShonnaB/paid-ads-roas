Data Quality & Gaps

Data sources

  • Spend (per-channel per-quarter) — platform-reported from the ad platforms (refresh 2026-06-23, full Q1 2025 → Q2 2026). Channels: Google Ads $382,929, Meta Ads $102,827, LinkedIn Ads $17,111, Microsoft Ads $3,000, Vibe $12,000. Grand total $517,867. Used for both per-channel and combined RoAS.
  • Impressions, clicks, CTR, CPC, sessions, leads — HubSpot Ads CSV (Oct 1, 2025 → June 23, 2026).
  • Pipeline, revenue, deal counts, dealstage — pulled live from HubSpot deal records, broken out by month / quarter / channel.
  • gclid + Team flag — manual spreadsheet pull of 87 closed-won deals (Jan 2025 → May 2026) whose primary contact had a Google Click ID. The Team field comes from LASSO's internal attribution (Team=Marketing = 46, Team=Sales = 41) — used as a "strong Direct" indicator. For these 87 deals, the contact's hs_analytics_first_timestamp was pulled directly to apply the 90-day Direct rule.

Attribution lenses (composite, v3)

  • Direct (372 closed deals) — Deals meeting any of these criteria:
    • point_of_conversion = "Paid" (rep-tagged).
    • First-touch source IN (PAID_SEARCH, PAID_SOCIAL). HubSpot locks first-touch on deal creation, so this implicitly means "before deal creation."
    • Last-touch source IN (PAID_SEARCH, PAID_SOCIAL) AND hs_analytics_latest_source_timestamp ≤ deal create date. Excludes post-creation drift.
  • Influenced (393 closed deals) — Everything in Direct, PLUS:
    • Last-touch source paid at any time (catches drift — last-touch changed to paid after deal create).
    • Verified gclid AND a verifiable paid-touch timestamp ≤ the earliest "deal-has-progressed" timestamp (MIN of Proposal Sent entry, Contract Sent entry, Closed Won, Closed Lost). We can verify the timestamp only when ft_source = PAID_SEARCH (use contact's first_timestamp) or lt_source = PAID_SEARCH (use lt_timestamp). If neither is paid, we don't know when the gclid was added and can't trust the timing — those deals are excluded.

Gclid validation result

  • Of 87 verified-gclid deals: 24 had ft_source = PAID_SEARCH so the contact's first_timestamp is a trustworthy gclid-touch proxy.
  • 0 were validated via lt_source = PAID_SEARCH.
  • 13 couldn't be validated via gclid, but are Influenced anyway via other criteria (PoC=Paid, FT paid, LT drift).
  • 12 manually added to Influenced via override (verified outside the rule). 5 prior overrides dropped off this refresh because PoC=Paid is now set in HubSpot.
  • 38 were excluded — no source evidence to anchor the gclid timing.

Known limitations

  • Bucketing. The top RoAS-by-quarter tiles use deal create quarter (matched to that quarter's spend). The four trend charts and the quarterly bar chart use close month/quarter (when revenue actually realized). The two views answer different questions.
  • Dashboard scope: closed deals only (won + lost), amount > $0. Open pipeline is excluded, and $0 "spam/junk" deals are filtered out (we dropped ~1,300 zero-amount deals, most of which were spam form-fills from Google Ads in July 2025 — real paid traffic but with no business value).
  • gclid presence only covers 87 closed-won deals. The spreadsheet was a CW-only pull from your VP of Marketing's HubSpot view (12-month lookback). For Closed Lost deals or for any deal not in that list, gclid is treated as absent. Re-export with stage filter widened to include CL to fill the gap.
  • First-touch timestamp uses a deal-level vs contact-level mix. HubSpot doesn't expose hs_analytics_first_timestamp as a deal property — only as a contact property. For the 87 verified-gclid deals we pulled it from the primary contact to apply the 90-day rule. For the other 1690 deals we trust HubSpot's convention that the deal's first-touch source value is locked at deal creation (so criterion 2 effectively means "FT paid before deal create" without an explicit timestamp check).
  • Last-touch drift is captured in Influenced. If hs_analytics_latest_source_timestamp > deal create date and the source is paid, the deal counts as Influenced (drift) but not Direct (because the paid touch happened post-create).
  • Meta/LinkedIn report $0 platform revenue in HubSpot Ads. HubSpot only attributes ad revenue cleanly for Google Ads in this account. Real Meta/LinkedIn impact shows up on the deal side via the attribution lenses.
  • Q2 2026 RoAS will mature. Many Q2-created deals haven't closed yet; the Q2 column reflects only deals that have already closed.
